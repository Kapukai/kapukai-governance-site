Data deletion instructions

For Kapukai Assistant, including its WhatsApp integration.

Request deletion by email

Email info@kapukai.org with the subject Kapukai data deletion request.

  1. Identify the Kapukai service you used.
  2. Provide the WhatsApp number, including country code, or email associated with that service.
  3. Tell us whether you want all associated personal data deleted or only particular records.

Do not send passwords, API keys, verification codes, identity documents, or case evidence with your initial request.

Verification and processing

Deletion requests require human review. We may ask you to confirm control of the account or number before acting, to prevent someone else from requesting deletion of your data. Sending a request does not itself delete records. Request a status update through the same email address.

Scope of your request

Requests can cover personal data held by Kapukai in connection with the assistant, such as contact identifiers, retained messages, and associated service records, where those records exist. Review includes identifying records held by service providers on Kapukai's behalf and any applicable deletion process.

Some records may need to be retained for applicable legal obligations, security, or dispute resolution. Any applicable exception, backup limitation, or provider retention limitation should be explained in the response to your request.

WhatsApp and AI service providers

The WhatsApp integration uses Meta to deliver messages and OpenAI to generate AI responses. Deleting Kapukai-held data does not automatically erase messages on your device, other recipients' copies, or records independently retained by providers. Those records are subject to the providers' own controls and policies.

Disconnecting is separate from deletion

You can stop messaging or block the assistant in WhatsApp. Removing a connection or deleting a chat on your device does not itself submit a request to delete data held by Kapukai. Use the email instructions above.
