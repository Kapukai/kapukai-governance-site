# Kapukai Civic Heritage — Brand System v1.0
Status: proposed brand system, subject to human approval, accessibility testing and asset-rights review. 2026-11-20.

## Identity
**Quiet authority. Human dignity. American public service.**
Primary public promise: **Government should work together. And work for you.**
Teaching line: **That's interoperability.**
Supporting public language: **We the People. United People.**
Formal technology: PDIP — Public Decision Integrity Protocol. Architecture: ACA. Application: Eagle Eyes. Map: Kapukai Atlas.
Intended outcome: Whole Person → Whole Family → Whole Community → Whole Society Flourishing (bidirectional effects, not deterministic).

## Palette (working tokens)
- Civic navy: `#142A45`
- Heritage red: `#A72E37`
- Warm ivory: `#F6F3EC`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Muted gold: `#B49A68`
Navy and ivory dominate; red signals emphasis; gold only restrained accents. Test all text/background combinations to WCAG 2.2 AA before release.

## Typography
Editorial serif for restrained headlines; readable sans for body, controls and diagrams. Inspect existing approved font licenses and exact logo before locking families. No invented K logo or government seal.

## Photography — documentary architectural recipe
Prefer authentic, licensed/commissioned photographs. Historic American architecture, physically plausible daylight, real people and genuine civic activity, meaningful American flags sparingly. Nikon D600 FX full-frame 24.3 MP as reference; Sigma 35mm f/1.4 DG HSM Art Nikon F mount or appropriate NIKKOR prime; choose focal length by scene; 14-bit RAW, ISO 100 where possible, tripod, f/5.6–f/8 where appropriate, highlight-preserving exposure, restrained grading. Use focus stacking only for critical near/far sharpness; exposure bracketing and panorama stitching are different techniques. D600 needs manual focus-step workflow for stacking. Generated images are conceptual and never claimed to be actual D600 captures or historical documentary evidence.
Avoid synthetic-looking montage, giant eagles, impossible Washington geometry, excessive HDR, glow, fake seals, distorted flags, patriotic spectacle.

## Image hierarchy
1. Real architectural hero with headline-safe negative space.
2. Real people and communities as purpose.
3. Whole Person Flourishing editable semantic diagram.
4. Three-branch authority map with jurisdictional boundaries.
5. PDIP send/acknowledge/review/authorized response/receipt/correction.
6. ACA source/authority/evidence/decision/impact/remedy proof map.
All diagrams must be editable, accessible and have text equivalents. Use synthetic case scenarios only.

## Motion and platform responsibilities

GitHub: versioned canonical text and implementation design tokens.

No automatic publishing, no private case evidence in public assets.

## Governance and political separation
Kapukai remains an independent civic engineering identity, nonpartisan in its standards. Any future personal Senate campaign needs a separate brand, organization, compliance review, disclosures and assets. Do not imply official government affiliation.
Review gates: verified logo/fonts, rights and source provenance, authentic image geography, accessible contrast, keyboard and screen reader, responsive crops, human approval.
