The whole person matters.

People are more than forms, files, and case numbers. Decisions can affect families, communities, and society. Our proposed framework aims to make consequential decisions understandable, traceable, and correctable.

Respecting constitutional boundaries

One public. Three branches. Different responsibilities.

Communication between institutions does not merge their powers, establish jurisdiction, or turn an allegation into a finding.

Legislative

Make and change laws

Constituent communication, legislation, and oversight—within applicable authority.

Executive

Administer and implement

Agency processes, authorized actions, records, and responses.

Judicial

Resolve legal questions

Filings, proceedings, orders, and review subject to court rules and due process.

Public Decision Integrity Protocol

Communication needs a return path.

PDIP is a proposed protocol for verifiable communication and correction. The stages below are a conceptual lifecycle, not a live government service.

  1. 1. Send
  2. 2. Acknowledge
  3. 3. Review
  4. 4. Authorized response
  5. 5. Action receipt
  6. 6. Correct or escalate

A receipt proves that something was received—not that its contents were verified or legally accepted. Every decision must remain bounded by the responsible authority's actual powers.

Learn the vocabulary

What is interoperability?

The ability of distinct systems to exchange and use information through shared rules while remaining independent.

What is ACA?

Authority Circuit Architecture is Kapukai's proposed way to map lawful authority, evidence, decisions, impacts, and remedies.

What is PDIP?

Public Decision Integrity Protocol is a proposed set of communication and integrity rules. It is not an adopted government standard.

Our commitment

Human dignity. Verifiable decisions. Meaningful correction.

This educational prototype uses no personal case records, has no data submission, and does not represent a government agency or offer legal advice.
