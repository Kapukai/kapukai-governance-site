05 / Method & accountability

Make every claim
checkable.

The collection is research infrastructure. Its assessment labels concern a stated proposition, not a person's credibility or legal status.

01

Preserve

Keep the original, record its provenance, and hash the file. Work from a copy. Record what was acquired and when.

02

Distinguish

Separate observation, testimony, allegation, expert interpretation, and official finding. Cite the exact page or passage.

03

Challenge

Record contradictions and ordinary alternative explanations. State what evidence would strengthen or weaken a hypothesis.

04

Correct

Version each change. Preserve the earlier wording, explain the correction, and update every affected summary and map.

06 / Public contribution

Add evidence.
Improve the record.

Start with a public source, a precise claim, and a citation another reader can inspect. Keep private case files in your own protected workspace.

What a useful contribution includes

  1. The case ID and exact statement to add or correct.
  2. A public URL, document title, date, and page or passage.
  3. What the source supports, and what it does not establish.
  4. Contradictory material and the reason for the proposed change.

GitHub issues and pull requests are public. Do not post private identifiers, a child's records, medical records, sealed material, passwords, or unredacted personal evidence.
