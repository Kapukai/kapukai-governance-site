01

The architectural question

Who owns the whole consequence?

An office may finish its assigned task while a person’s problem remains unresolved. Responsibility for a step is not the same as responsibility for the outcome.

Protective separation

Independent review, limited access to sensitive data, and separation of incompatible duties can contain error and abuse.

Harmful fragmentation

A warning or accepted correction has no accountable recipient, deadline, acknowledgment, or route to resolve disagreement.

Engineering basis: NIST systems principles, STPA interaction and feedback analysis, and GAO internal controls. Application to this public-administration map is Kapukai’s analysis.

Kapukai’s hypothesis to test

Asymmetry can become structural.

If enforcement is funded, connected and measured while correction is not, the design may reproduce that imbalance. Establish it by comparing matched cases, resources, deadlines and completed outcomes.

Intent is a separate question. Differences between blueprints can reveal priorities. A claim of coordinated harm requires additional evidence of knowledge, choices, communications and consequences, tested against other explanations.

Coupling, coherence and amplification: make the terms measurable
Coupling / decoupling
How a change in one office affects another. Trace both adverse actions and corrections: who receives them, acknowledges them and acts? Keep independent judgment and privacy boundaries where they protect people.
Coherence / incoherence
Whether records, reasons and actions remain compatible with their sources and effective dates. “Decoherence” is an analogy here, not physics. Agreement alone is not truth: many systems can copy one wrong source.
Amplification / damping
Whether a mistake spreads or is contained. These are the operational terms for the proposed “resonance / deresonance” analogy. Count downstream effects, stale reuse and time until new consequences stop.
Correction propagation
Whether an accepted correction reaches its recipients and changes their behavior. A sent message is not an updated record; an updated record is not necessarily restored service.

Preserve the origin of each claim and distinguish independent evidence from repeated copies. W3C PROV provides a framework for recording provenance.

Less burden. More effective protection.

Every requirement needs a job.

Minimize the work a person must do while preserving necessary safety, rights, privacy and correction. A rule count alone cannot tell us whether a system is safe.

Across agencies, domains and nations

Compare functions before institutions.

Use a shared audit structure with local law and domain-specific safety thresholds. A benefits decision, emergency dispatch and custody transfer have different exposures, timescales and consequences. Do not combine them into one unexplained danger score.

Publish two heatmaps

Control evidence: documented, operating, independently tested, failed, or unassessed. Burden: measured time, cost and duplication. Keep evidence quality visible beside both.

Compare like with like

Declare scope, period, legal duties, severity, workload, case mix, reporting completeness and denominators. A low recorded incident rate may reflect missing reports. Fewer rules do not establish lower risk.

The WHO patient-safety observatory offers an example of structured implementation indicators; these are not interchangeable with independently verified outcome rates. International comparison is a proposed next phase. This publication does not contain a harmonized multi-country dataset and does not rank nations or agencies.

06

A repair architecture

Fewer dead ends.
Proof that the fix reached people.

For a person

A clear entry point; reasons and accessible evidence; a route to urgent protection where authorized; independent review; an owner who follows correction through to its effects.

For the system

Trace the failed control, identify every affected interface, assign a responsible owner, test the repair and monitor recurrence. An audit finding or closed ticket is an intermediate result.

Courts and hearing bodies can provide review within their jurisdiction. Inspectors general and auditors examine failures. Agency leaders operate services and implement corrections. Legislatures and rulemakers change underlying authority. These functions connect, but they are not interchangeable. Open the courts, DMV, SSA and 911 relationship atlas →

Kapukai proposal

A public decision safety assurance function.

Give cross-agency repair an accountable coordinator, documented authority and a verified completion record. Preserve independent review, limited access, privacy, conflict controls and appealable findings. A new office is useful only if it reduces dead ends without becoming another gate.

Can a system prevent and correct harm as reliably as it exercises power?
Measure avoidable work, effective protection and completed repair together.

Scope, evidence quality and corrections

Edition 1 · 16 October 2026. The heatmap is a purposive set of eight examples, not a representative sample. Requirements, historical findings, reported reforms and measured outcomes are different evidence types. No total score, current danger ranking, corruption estimate or national league table is inferred.

The blueprints and measurement framework are Kapukai analysis and proposals. Official sources support the specific attributed findings, not every broader inference. Historical problems may have been remediated after the stated review; the map says when current effectiveness is unverified.

To report a source error, identify the publication section, source and proposed correction through Kapukai’s contact page. Start with public information; do not submit private case files. This research does not extend filing deadlines or provide an emergency-response channel.
