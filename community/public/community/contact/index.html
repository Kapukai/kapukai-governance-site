One place to find us

Contact the lab.

For questions about tools, learning, participation or your data: architect@kapukai.org.

Please start with a general question. Do not send case files, identity documents, names of children or private addresses. In-person visits require a confirmed appointment.

Find the right next step

  • Request member access or accept an invitation
  • Ask about free or discounted support
  • Apply as a reviewer or observation volunteer
  • Choose class, volunteer or collaboration notices
  • Request access, withdrawal or deletion assistance

Visits and correspondence

Contact architect@kapukai.org for an appointment or mailing instructions before sending documents.

Please do not arrive without a confirmed appointment or email private records without an agreed process.
