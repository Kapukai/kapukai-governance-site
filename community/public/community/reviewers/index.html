Careful work · Clear limits

Contribute what you can responsibly observe.

Apply to help with independent review, source checking or a defined observation role.

Two different roles

Independent reviewer applicant

Express interest in checking a defined process, source or output. Relevant training, competence and conflicts must be assessed for each assignment.

Independent observation volunteer

Document what you personally observed within an agreed scope. Hearing someone describe an event does not establish that the event occurred.

  1. Apply without private case information.
    A professional category and broad availability are enough to start.
  2. Confirm your email.
    This starts human review, not an assignment.
  3. Agree the scope before participating.
    Training, conflicts, confidentiality, participant consent and supervision are separate onboarding steps.
