About the Workshop

Engineering people can inspect.

Kapukai Governance Lab works on public decision systems: how records become classifications, obligations and consequential decisions, and how errors can be corrected.

Our working order

  1. Safety, consent and control.
  2. Successful participation.
  3. Useful human connection.
  4. Sustainable maintenance.
  5. Learning and correction.
  6. Growth that preserves those priorities.

What we offer

Engineering tools, document organization and formatting, educational resources, research and model policy drafts. Any draft remains subject to the user’s review and decision about use. An attorney or public official may review it independently.

Kapukai is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice, representation or a guarantee of a legal result.

How we learn

Define the problem and scope. Identify stakeholders. Set priorities. Build a small, testable improvement. Measure useful outcomes, privacy comprehension and maintenance costs. Record what changed, then keep, adjust or reverse it.
