Access within our capacity

Cost should not end the conversation.

Ask about free or discounted help with tools, learning or engineering support. A person reviews each confirmed application.

  1. Tell us the kind of help you need.
    Choose a broad topic. You do not need to explain a personal case.
  2. Confirm your email.
    Opening the link alone does not submit the confirmed application. Review it and choose Confirm.
  3. Wait for a human response.
    We will discuss what is possible within available capacity before you decide whether to proceed.
No proof of hardship required at this stage.
No pay stubs, ID scans or private records. Testing, volunteering, publicity and positive feedback are not conditions of assistance.

Possible support may begin with learning resources or a limited tool test. Engineering time, independent review and other services depend on scope and capacity. There is no promised turnaround.

Kapukai provides engineering and formatting support, not legal advice or representation. Nothing here extends a filing deadline or arranges emergency assistance.

Private request · Adults 18+

Ask about support

We will send a private confirmation link. Confirming your email places this application in the human review process.

For this application only. No newsletter subscription.

A legal name is not required. Use an alias without private details.

Before you submit

Do not provide pay stubs, identity documents, case files, private addresses or names of children. There is no upload field.
