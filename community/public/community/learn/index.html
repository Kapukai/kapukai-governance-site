Your learning journey

Know what happens next.

  1. Explore.

    Find a skill in the classroom. Read what is available and what is still being prepared.

  2. Try.

    Use the free sample exercise or fictional practice cases. No signup is needed for those resources.

  3. Choose.

    Optional updates announce opportunities. A specific session has its own registration, capacity and access terms.

  4. Attend and practice.

    Use your session confirmation and the instructor’s materials. Dates, recordings and support are stated for each session.

  5. Check and improve.

    Use the exercise rubric when supplied. Report a broken link or learning-material correction through the Workshop contact route.
