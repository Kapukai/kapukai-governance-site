What is available?

This is the Workshop’s feature availability guide, not a real-time uptime monitor. Check the response to a form before assuming a request was received.

Available

Learning & interest lists

  • Fictional practice cases without signup.
  • Public article and source references.
  • Separate email choices for classes, volunteering, collaboration, testing and The Remedy Brief.
  • Email confirmation and withdrawal through your private link.
Subject to human review

Applications

  • Free or discounted assistance.
  • Reviewer or observation volunteer interest.
  • Email confirmation starts review; it does not grant service access.
  • Submission may be paused when capacity is unavailable. The form will tell you if it cannot accept a request.

  • Interest pools, mutual discovery and introductions.
  • Reviewer matching, shared calendars and case assignments.
  • Public feedback publication and expert credentials.
