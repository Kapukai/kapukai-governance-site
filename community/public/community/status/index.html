Clear expectations

What is available?

This is the Workshop’s feature availability guide, not a real-time uptime monitor. Check the response to a form before assuming a request was received.

Available

Learning & interest lists

  • First Circle classroom preview and webinar calendar.
  • Fictional practice cases without signup.
  • Public article and source references.
  • Separate email choices for classes, volunteering, collaboration, testing and The Remedy Brief.
  • Email confirmation and withdrawal through your private link.
Subject to human review

Applications

  • Free or discounted assistance.
  • Reviewer or observation volunteer interest.
  • Email confirmation starts human review; it does not grant service access.
  • Your private application link shows progress, structured requests, free resource offers, delivery, feedback and correction.
  • Understand the application lifecycle. Human decisions and capacity remain required.
  • Submission may be paused when capacity is unavailable. The form will tell you if it cannot accept a request.
Invitation-only pilot

Member community

  • Verified accounts, private profiles and three rooms you choose to join.
  • Optional interest discovery and introductions accepted by both people.
  • Scoped review requests, accepted assignments and private shared appointments.
  • Contributor-approved feedback and human-checked credentials, released separately.
Member sign in

Up to 30 invited adults. No case files, automated expert status or public member directory. View released contributions.

Coming later

Classes & guided work

Dates, registration, learning materials and any course requirements will be announced separately. There are no confirmed events listed here.

Explore planned learning formats
Your choices remain separate.
An application does not subscribe you to a newsletter, publish your profile or authorize case sharing. Room membership, discovery, appointments and public release each have separate controls.
