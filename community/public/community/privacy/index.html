Signup privacy

Your choices stay separate.

Kapukai Governance Lab collects your email address, selected email purposes, tool interests when supplied, newsletter frequency, and the times and versions of confirmation or withdrawal. We use these records to carry out your request and document your choices.

What this form does

Tester invitations, The Remedy Brief, volunteer opportunities, free class and webinar notices, and collaboration follow-ups are separate permissions. Dates and volunteer assignments are announced separately. Joining one does not subscribe you to the other. A purchase, download or interest in testing does not authorize public sharing of your story.

How confirmation works

Postmark delivers a confirmation link. Your selected purposes become active only after you confirm them. The confirmation page also offers withdrawal. Keep that private link to manage its choices, or contact us for help. A new request cannot silently add permissions to an existing address.

What we do not collect here

This signup has no case upload or story field. Do not send names of children, case numbers, court files, private addresses or sensitive narratives. Downloaded fictional practice cases need no signup.

Storage and your requests

Supabase holds these records in Kapukai’s existing project. Postmark handles delivery. Access is restricted to the service and authorized operators. This page uses no advertising pixels or optional behavioral analytics.

For withdrawal, access or deletion assistance, email architect@kapukai.org. We may retain the minimum suppression and consent history needed to honor your choices. Confirmation links expire after 48 hours. A valid link can withdraw its selected purposes for up to 180 days. Expiring a link does not delete the underlying record. Contact us for a data request; we retain the minimum consent and suppression history needed to honor your choices. Do not use this form to submit a legal emergency.

Notice version: kapukai-scoped-v2-2026-10-08.

Assistance and contributor applications

Application notice version: kapukai-applications-v1-2026-10-08.

The separate application form collects your email, optional alias, application type, selected service interest, requested support where relevant, optional broad professional category and availability, and your acknowledgements. It records request, confirmation and withdrawal information, structured preferences, human decisions, resource offers, delivery versions, receipt acknowledgements, feedback, correction requests and notification attempts so authorized operators can follow the work and document your choices.

We use your email to send the confirmation link and contact you about this application. Applying does not subscribe you to a newsletter, publish a profile or authorize sharing private case information. There is no automated decision about the merits of your situation. Confirmed applications wait for human review and available capacity.

Supabase stores the application records in Kapukai’s existing project. Postmark delivers application emails. Access is restricted to the service and authorized operators. Do not submit pay stubs, identity documents, case histories, private addresses or names of children. A legal name is not required. Use an alias without private details.

The confirmation link can confirm a request for 48 hours and manage this application for up to 180 days. After confirmation, the private page can show human review progress, ask for structured clarification, present a free nonbinding resource offer, acknowledge delivery, collect optional structured feedback, and receive correction or reconsideration requests. These choices and decision times form a private application history. Accepting an offer is not a purchase, contract, assignment, credential or public release. Feedback is not published, and no case narrative or file upload is accepted. Opening a link does not confirm an application. Withdrawal ends this application’s active review request; it does not erase necessary consent and audit history or change separate email subscriptions. Contact architect@kapukai.org for access or deletion assistance. Separate onboarding and consent are required before any assignment or private record sharing.
