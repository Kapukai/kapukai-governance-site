How joining works

One step at a time.

  1. Request access. Give your email and display name. No password or invitation is needed.
  2. Confirm your request. Open the email and choose Confirm my access request.
  3. Wait for a person to review it. The operator approves, then previews and sends your private invitation email.
  4. Use your invitation. Create an account, enter the separate email verification code, and join. Existing accounts can sign in and accept the invitation.

Approving people requires an operator account. After signing in, operators see Pilot operations.
