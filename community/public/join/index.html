What kind of work would you enjoy?

Change the Board is part of the nationwide Family Rights Movement. Pick an area to see a few ways to help.

Help at a local event
  • Welcome and volunteer captain — welcome people and coordinate volunteer shifts
  • Accessibility and first-aid contact — help plan accessible routes, water and on-site support
  • Logistics and documentation — help with supplies, setup and cleanup
Observe a public hearing
  • Court watcher — observe an open hearing and take neutral notes where permitted
  • Court watch captain — coordinate observers and review their reports
Help from home
  • Social media and design — prepare approved graphics and posts
  • Legal research and legislative outreach — check public sources and research proposals
  • Data and organization — help maintain opt-in schedules and aggregate counts
