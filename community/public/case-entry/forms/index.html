03 / Evidence and handoffs

Who can provide what?

Published steps and opportunities are shown separately from observed events. Read the cited sources for their conditions and branches.

    Documented evidence channels

    Submission, acceptance, availability to a decision maker, consideration and factual findings are separate events. A recorded file hash establishes byte integrity; it does not establish the truth of its contents.

    04 / Money and access

    What does participation require?

    Published charges, upfront requirements and actual expense have separate fields. An unknown amount stays unknown.

    Required upfront

    The payment needed before a particular step can proceed, including the scope of any assistance or alternative.

    Actual expense

    What a participant spent, with the currency, category, source and observation period retained.

    Unable to proceed

    A separately documented access barrier. The absence of a completed step does not establish its cause.

    A published $0 court charge does not establish $0 total participation cost.

    05 / Time and review

    Which clock applies?

    Record the trigger, effective date and jurisdiction. Published review windows are not measurements of actual court performance.

    06 / Observed operation

    What happened in practice?

    This edition contains public-source descriptions. Actual submission rates, timing and participant spending remain unmeasured.

    Future rates need a population, observation window, denominator and missing-data count. Unfinished cases must remain visible.
