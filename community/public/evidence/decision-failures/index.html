The question behind this work

Who is accountable for the design of a consequential decision? These cases document failures of records, notice, oversight or institutional practice. Publicly inspectable testing, evidence and accountable review are Kapukai proposals. The cases do not establish absence of an engineer as the sole cause.

03 / Inspect, test, improve

Every finding needs a source.
Every remedy has a scope.

What is included

Selected public opinions, official investigations and court-entered remedies. Each record distinguishes allegations, preliminary decisions, final holdings and settlements. Historical findings do not certify current compliance.

What names mean

Judges may have ordered a correction. An official may be named only in an official capacity. Police personnel may have discovered or relayed an error. Read the role and source before drawing a conclusion.

What engineering adds

Kapukai’s analysis asks which control failed, who owned it, how it could be tested, and whether a correction reached every affected decision. Professional credentials alone cannot establish that a system is safe.

Coverage and corrections

No cases in a state means not yet indexed. This is a reviewed publication snapshot, not live docket monitoring. To propose a correction, identify the case, source passage and proposed change through Kapukai’s contact page. Do not send private case files.

A result needs its scope.

Publish the claim and governing requirement; system and data versions; population, sample and denominator; method and acceptance threshold; date, result and uncertainty; tester and reviewer; limitations and reproduction steps; and when the result was last checked. Identify what was not tested or measured. Missing data is not a zero error rate.

Each case shows Kapukai’s own verification status. Document review, operational testing and current compliance are separate activities. NIST’s measurement guidance calls for documenting unmeasured risks, test sets, metrics and evaluation methods.

What assurance means

Assurance is established engineering terminology: evidence and reasoning supporting a specified claim. An assurance case does not provide unequivocal proof or a guarantee. Statistical intervals and tests may contribute evidence, but a signature or a single confidence score cannot replace stated scope, methods and limits.

Where the data lives

Structured case, source and role records are stored in access-controlled Supabase. This site reads a versioned public export, available as JSON, CSV and SQLite. Source code and public editions are on GitHub. This is not a live docket feed.
