A thoughtful place to work.

The welcome and waiting area is available before opening. During class, quiet tea and soda are welcome. Food and other refreshments are for lunch, before opening, or after closing in the designated area.

Bring a purpose, not a dossier.

Booking starts with room and setup details. Keep private case facts, identity documents, and sensitive records out of booking details.

Visits are arranged directly. This public overview does not accept case records, reservations, or payments. Private workspace access remains separate.
