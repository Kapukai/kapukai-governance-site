Kapukai Tester Circle

Help make useful
tools better.

Request free testing invitations for affidavit drafting, timeline and evidence organization tools. Start with fictional cases and tell us what needs to work better.

  1. Choose your invitations.
    Confirm your email and keep newsletter permission separate.
  2. Wait for a suitable test.
    An invitation will explain access, scope and what feedback is useful.
  3. Use fictional practice cases.
    Check the tool without sharing a real family’s records.
Joining is an application, not immediate tool access. No payment to join. Test outputs are practice drafts, not documents approved for filing.
