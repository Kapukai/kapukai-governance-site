What the stage labels mean

Educational prototype: an illustrative or synthetic demonstration.

Preparation preview: explore with fictional material; sensitive-record workflows are not verified.

Invited pilot: testing or access requires separate approval.

Research framework: a proposal to inspect and test, not an adopted standard or certification.

Public resource: available to read; forms and services have their own availability.

Earlier reference / Source project: dated research or code that needs current review before use.
