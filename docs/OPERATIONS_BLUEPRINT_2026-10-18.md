# Kapukai participation and operational remedy

## Ownership
Supabase owns signup scopes, confirmation, withdrawal and suppression. HubSpot owns human follow-up and commercial opportunities. Postmark supplies transport receipts, never proof of fulfilled service. Operational commitments are separate per purpose and retain dated events. Do not copy case narratives, identity documents, private tokens or secrets into CRM notes.

## Person and commitment
Reuse existing contact IDs. A contact can have several commitments: engineer, technician, tester, evaluation, research or supporter. Interest is not qualification, assignment or marketing permission. Existing signups are not backfilled with inferred purposes. Record owner, next action, due date and source; unknown attribution remains unknown.

Stages: interest, conversation, agreed, in_progress, completed, paused, withdrawn. Completion requires an evidence reference. Withdrawn work requires fresh authorization before restarting. A revision check prevents stale changes; request IDs prevent duplicate events. Evidence references identify protected records; they must not embed sensitive narratives.

## Recovery boundaries
Existing CRM worker performs bounded retries, lease checks, account verification and independent readback. The operations monitor runs independently every five minutes and opens durable incidents for missing worker heartbeat, credential/configuration errors, blocked or overdue sync, and overdue commitments. Repeated detections update one incident. State changes retain history. Recovered technical conditions resolve their technical incident; a paused or withdrawn commitment ends an overdue condition without proving fulfilled service.

No automatic qualification, eligibility, grant, substantive adverse decision or outbound outreach. Uncertain email handoffs require provider reconciliation before any resend. Human decisions remain explicit. Owner for initial operational incidents: existing active HubSpot owner 90662861.

## Integration release gates
1. Existing signup consent/withdrawal behavior remains intact.
2. Extend forms with optional self-selected participation purposes; preserve existing purposes.
3. Capture allowlisted campaign identifiers only, never full sensitive URLs or private fragments.
4. Confirm participation interests before projecting them; do not infer from general volunteer signup.
5. Associate tasks with existing contacts and explicit commitments; reconcile uncertain task creates before retry.
6. Commercial deals begin only for concrete opportunities. Payment is separate from deal acceptance.
7. Use existing navy/gold/white HTML shell and text alternatives. Lotus asset must be verified before replacing the current monogram.
8. Public UI publication must use the existing kapukai.org host; no DNS replacement of the apex.
