KAPUKAI GOVERNANCE LAB — QUESTIONS FOR PENSION TRUSTEES
Research request framework, not an allegation or legal demand.

1. Does the fund have direct or indirect exposure to data brokers, consumer reporting businesses, or businesses licensing personal information? Please identify the reporting date, manager, vehicle and source for each disclosed holding, subject to applicable disclosure rules.
2. What privacy, data provenance and permissible-use due diligence does the fund require from managers?
3. Who is responsible for reviewing credible complaints and regulatory findings concerning portfolio businesses?
4. What governance rights does the fund actually possess? Distinguish limited-partner rights from operating authority.
5. What measures verify correction, suppression and downstream compliance after a confirmed problem?
6. Can trustees publish aggregate review results without exposing affected people's private records?

A financial relationship alone does not prove participation in unlawful conduct.
Do not attach protected addresses, children's identities, sealed materials, or private case files to a general inquiry.
