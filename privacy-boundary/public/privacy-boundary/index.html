SECONDARY USE

“We can use this data.”

A recipient repurposes, matches, discloses, or sells it. Identify the actual use and test the claimed authority against its limits.

Access to data is not proof of authority for every use.

What the original explanation gets wrong

Public service records are not all public records. Confidentiality rules and disclosure exemptions depend on the record and jurisdiction. The Driver’s Privacy Protection Act restricts disclosure and reuse; its exceptions are purpose-specific. A private investigator’s license is not unlimited permission. Bulk DMV marketing disclosure under §2721(b)(12) requires express consent.

Secondary use is not automatically identity theft. “Data Sovereign Encapsulation” is not an established legal remedy. Zero-knowledge proofs can reduce disclosure in selected designs, but cannot eliminate every agency’s need to retain identity records. New taxes, automatic payouts, and universal opt-in rules are policy proposals, not existing nationwide remedies.

LAW → FACTS → POSSIBLE REMEDY

Choose the rule
that fits the evidence.

A troubling practice and an actionable claim are different questions. Start with the data source and actual use. Confirm current law, controlling decisions, deadlines, and the proper forum with counsel.

01Motor vehicle records DPPA

Boundary. 18 U.S.C. §2721 restricts personal information obtained from motor vehicle records. Permitted uses and redisclosures have conditions; research and marketing are not unrestricted exceptions.

Proof. Identify the motor vehicle source, the individual’s information, the defendant’s knowing acquisition, disclosure or use, and why that purpose was not permitted. A matching address alone does not establish the source.

Route. §2724 permits a civil action and specified damages, fees and equitable relief when requirements are met. An award is not automatic. §2725’s definition of “person” excludes a State or state agency; defendant selection and immunity require separate analysis.

§2721 · §2724 · §2725

02Eligibility and background reports FCRA

Boundary. When the Fair Credit Reporting Act applies, permissible purpose, accuracy and dispute obligations matter. Coverage depends on the actor, report and use; not every people-search listing is a consumer report.

Proof. Preserve the actual report, recipient, screening purpose, inaccurate item, dispute correspondence and any adverse-action notice.

Route. Civil remedies depend on the violated duty and whether noncompliance was negligent or willful. A reporting error alone does not establish every element.

§1681b: purpose · §1681e: procedures · §1681i: disputes · §1681n / §1681o: liability
03Public data and speech FIRST AMENDMENT

Boundary. Publication and data use can receive constitutional protection. In Sorrell v. IMS Health, 564 U.S. 552 (2011), the Supreme Court invalidated Vermont’s restrictions on prescriber-identifying information under heightened scrutiny.

Limit. That holding is not a universal license for unlawful access, prohibited disclosures, deception, or every downstream use. A proposed restriction must address the applicable constitutional analysis.

Read the opinion ↗
04Your harm and the court’s power STANDING

Boundary. Federal court requires a concrete, particularized injury fairly traceable to the defendant and likely redressable by the requested relief. TransUnion LLC v. Ramirez, 594 U.S. 413 (2021), distinguishes a statutory violation from concrete harm.

Proof. Identify dissemination, denial, financial loss, reputational injury or another legally cognizable harm. Future-risk and injunction theories require their own analysis. State court standing can differ.

PENSION FUNDS · PRIVATE EQUITY · PARENT COMPANIES

Follow the money.
Then prove the conduct.

An investment relationship is an investigative lead. It does not, by itself, establish that an investor obtained data, directed a sale, or caused an injury.

Build the ownership record.

Obtain dated fund disclosures, portfolio reports, corporate filings, acquisition records and governance documents. Separate a pension beneficiary, limited partner, investment manager, parent company and operating business.

Build the responsibility record.

Identify the specific entity’s acts, decisions, instructions and knowledge. Counsel must test direct liability, agency, or any veil-piercing theory under the applicable law. Public funds may raise immunity and forum issues.

United States v. Bestfoods, 524 U.S. 51 (1998), explains corporate separateness and direct versus derivative liability in a CERCLA case. It is useful background, not a data-privacy cause of action. Read the decision ↗

Evidence preservation checklist
  • Retain original files, dated URLs, complete reports and correspondence. Record when and how each item was obtained.
  • Keep a separate exhibit index with author, custodian, date, page and the proposition supported.
  • Mark firsthand knowledge, third-party statements, inference and unknowns distinctly.
  • Preserve evidence before requesting deletion where feasible; ask counsel about preservation notices and urgent protection.
  • Keep a private original and a separately redacted sharing copy. Authentication and admissibility require case-specific review.
