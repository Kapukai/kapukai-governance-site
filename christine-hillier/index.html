Professional profile

Christine Hillier

Founder & Systems Architect · Kapukai Governance Lab

Christine Hillier works at the intersection of systems architecture, governance engineering, and public-interest technology. She founded Kapukai Governance Lab to develop approaches that make consequential decisions more inspectable, traceable, testable, and correctable.

Current work

Her work includes Authority Circuit Architecture (ACA), an open-standards initiative exploring how authority, applicable rules, supporting evidence, decision state, proof records, and remedy pathways can be represented and evaluated. ACA is a working draft under development, not a certified or deployed standard.
