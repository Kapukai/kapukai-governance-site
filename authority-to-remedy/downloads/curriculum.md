# Authority to Remedy Trace
## Module 01: Trace one consequential decision

**Kapukai Governance Lab · Foundation module · Facilitator edition · Version 1.0, October 9, 2026**

**Teaching promise:** Learners leave able to build and defend a sourced trace from one action or omission to an appropriate procedure and a verifiable remedy. A completed trace can establish uncertainty or a missing route; it need not assert that a violation occurred.

**Unit of analysis:** One actor exercising a particular power, through one action or omission, with a defined consequence for another person.

**Five-step method:** **Record → Classify → Invoke documented duty → Seek authorized enforcement → Verify remedy.**

“Invoke duty” is the operational meaning of “obligate” in this course: identify an obligation already established by an applicable source and satisfy the procedure that activates it. Private allegations, notices, affidavits and declarations do not create court powers, automatic debts, consent or sanctions. “Seek enforcement” means use an authorized route; it does not give the learner enforcement powers.

### Scope and teaching boundaries

This is an educational navigation and evidence course, not individualized legal representation. Every person, agency, rule, date and deadline in the three case packets is fictional and marked as such. Actual practice requires current, jurisdiction-specific sources and appropriate professional review when needed.

Use only synthetic cases in group work. Do not bring real child records, recordings, case numbers, medical details, names or identifying family narratives. A learner may pass on speaking or take an observer role without explanation. Do not require personal disclosure to demonstrate skill. Explain how participants can privately ask a process question without turning class into an intake session.

“Record” means preserving information lawfully obtained. Audio and video recording, access to restricted records and courtroom recording have their own applicable rules. The course does not supply permission to record a person or proceeding.

### Learning outcomes

By the end of homework and class, a learner can:

1. Write a neutral, verifiable sentence identifying one action or omission, its actor, date and consequence.
2. Separate role authentication, authority to take the particular action, required predicates and constitutional or procedural constraints.
3. Read an identified source into a Duty Card: actor, act, trigger, deadline, exceptions and evidence needed.
4. Classify duties as mandatory, conditional mandatory, discretionary, proposed assurance control or unknown, and distinguish a required decision from a preferred outcome.
5. Map the appropriate recipient, document, delivery or service requirement, deadline and source-specific review route without inventing jurisdiction.
6. Define evidence of remedy at the source record, known recipients, dependent decisions and practical outcome, distinguishing legal requirements from recommended verification.

### The repeatable learning objects

| Object | What it contains | What it must not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Action Trace | One act or omission; actor; date; consequence; evidence; uncertainty | That an unfavorable result proves misconduct |
| Authority Chain | Relevant constitutional structure; enabling source; role; scope; predicates; constraints | That the Declaration itself supplies every remedy or all state power flows through federal agencies |
| Duty Card | Source; actor; required act; activating facts; timing; exceptions; status | That a private notice creates an obligation |
| Route Card | Act challenged; review scheme; recipient or forum; required vehicle; prerequisite; deadline; relief | That every government issue is an APA or section 1983 case |
| Document Sequence | Inputs, outputs, responsible actor and branch conditions at each procedural checkpoint | A universal filing ladder or guarantee of jurisdiction |
| Remedy Receipt | Evidence of the ordered or required change and its practical implementation | That a favorable letter alone repairs every consequence |

## Homework: a short lesson with a usable output

**Assigned before the meeting:** approximately 14–16 minutes of video including pauses, followed by 8–12 minutes to complete a one-page trace. Provide the same script as readable text, a captioned version, and downloadable slides. Learners may complete the reading instead of the video. No account, autoplay audio or music is necessary to access the lesson.

**Homework packet:** video or transcript, slides, fictional Case A, and the one-page worksheet below. Give the packet at least 48 hours before class where feasible. Include the deadline in plain local date and time.

### Homework instructions for the learner

1. Watch or read Module 01. Pause when invited; longer pauses are welcome.
2. Read Case A and complete an Action Trace, a Duty Card, and the two deadline lines.
3. Write one sentence stating what the evidence supports and one sentence stating what remains unknown.
4. Bring one route question to class. Do not research or bring a personal legal matter for this exercise.

### Five-question retrieval check

Use questions as a self-check, not a gate excluding learners from class.

1. A person holds a verified official title. Does that establish authority for every action they take? **No. Trace the specific power and conditions.**
2. Three reports repeat the same spreadsheet error. Are these three independent sources? **No. Preserve source genealogy.**
3. The source says the agency must decide. Does that mean it must approve? **No. Required act and outcome are separate.**
4. A correction request receives an acknowledgement. Does it pause an appeal clock? **Only if an applicable rule makes it do so; the Case A rule says it does not.**
5. A source record is corrected. Is every consequence necessarily repaired? **No. Check known recipients, dependent decisions and practical implementation, while distinguishing required actions from recommended checks.**

## In-person class: 90 minutes

Recommended group size is five. Groups of four combine the fact-recorder and timekeeping functions; groups of six add an evidence custodian. Rotate roles so a participant does not stay in the role closest to their existing profession. Provide large-print worksheets and writable digital equivalents.

| Time | Activity | Facilitator action | Learner output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–8 min | Retrieval and orientation | State fictional-case boundary; retrieve five key distinctions; normalize a supported “unknown” | One corrected misconception |
| 8–18 min | Live trace demonstration | Read one short source aloud; annotate actor, act, trigger, clock and exception; show power vs constraint | Shared sample Duty Card |
| 18–38 min | First team exercise | Assign Cases A, B and C across teams; distribute evidence cards; ask teams to identify the narrowest supported issue | Action Trace, Duty Card, Route Card, two-clock register |
| 38–50 min | Independent cross-review | Exchange output with another team; reviewer must identify one strength, one unsupported inference and one missing source | Annotated trace with source-based challenges |
| 50–67 min | New evidence and role rotation | Rotate roles; supply the case-specific change card; require a revision log | Revised classification, request and remedy check |
| 67–79 min | Procedure simulation | Each team gets 2 minutes to request relief and 2 minutes to answer source/authority questions; use three teams or parallel stations | Bounded oral request supported by the written card |
| 79–85 min | Remedy closure exercise | Give one partial-completion result; ask what actually changed and what remains outstanding | Remedy Receipt with open gaps |
| 85–90 min | Individual exit proof | Distribute the short exit case; collect individual answer, including an honest unknown | Demonstrated individual skill, not just team performance |

### Team roles

| Role | Responsibilities | Mandatory question |
|---|---|---|
| Fact recorder | Maintains chronology and evidence IDs; distinguishes facts, quotations and inferences | “Where does the record show that?” |
| Authority researcher | Reads supplied sources; distinguishes power, duty, condition, exception and proposed safeguard | “Which source supports this exact power or duty?” |
| Procedural navigator | Identifies recipient, forum, filing vehicle, delivery, service, clock and missing prerequisites | “What must be true for this route to work?” |
| Independent reviewer | Challenges copied evidence, unsupported intent, scope errors and proposed relief beyond authority | “What evidence or rule would change our conclusion?” |
| Affected-person perspective / timekeeper | Checks whether the proposed remedy addresses the actual consequence, privacy and access; keeps stages on time | “What would have to change in daily life for this remedy to work?” |

The affected-person role is an analytic role using supplied facts. Do not ask learners to act out abuse, imitate distress, impersonate a real child or invent the child's preferences. Everyone may reject an unsupported factual addition.

## The three practice cases

The complete machine-readable evidence packets and answer keys are in `classroom_cases.json`. The versions below provide facilitator-ready summaries. Every numbered day is a **training day**, not an actual legal computation rule.

### Case A — A copied record changes the wrong family's status

**Current day D8.** A D0 agency notice changes family account R-184's transport priority based on three missed-pickup incidents. The original log identifies R-814. Three subsequent summaries all quote the same imported spreadsheet. A sufficiently identified correction request was received D6. A transport contractor received the inaccurate flag. The internal-review deadline is D15. The correction determination is due D16. A correction request does not pause the review clock. If the records officer confirms an error, the fictional rule requires correction and notice to known recipients within three days. An interim suspension is discretionary. No judicial route is supplied.

**Evidence packet:** A1 adverse notice; A2 original log; A3 derivative summary; A4 correction request and receipt; A5 downstream receipt; A6 fictional rule card.

**Expected work:** Identify the documented mismatch without claiming fraudulent intent; preserve the D15 internal-review route separately from the D16 correction response; request interim relief without treating it as automatic; describe how correction reaches the contractor and how the transport decision is separately reconsidered. Mark judicial review as a research gap.

**Change card at minute 50:** On D12, the records officer confirms and corrects the source error. On D14, the contractor still displays the flag. The correction letter does not say whether recipient notification was sent. The D15 internal-review deadline remains in effect. What must be checked now?

**Facilitator key:** Under TR-A4, notification is due D15. On D14 it is not yet overdue. Request or check notification evidence and continue to preserve the review route by D15. The source correction is one completed step; it is not proof of operational correction or proof that all procedural clocks disappeared. Contractor acknowledgement is a useful check but is not an additional legal duty established by the packet.

**Partial-remedy card at minute 79:** “The file has been annotated.” Ask whether the active erroneous field changed, the decision was reconsidered and known recipients were notified. A vague annotation is insufficient evidence of those changes.

### Case B — A required service cannot be accessed

**Current day D12.** A D0 fictional court order requires Jordan to contact the agency by D3 and attend an available approved course within D30. The agency must make an approved course available without participant cost by D7. Jordan contacts the agency D2. The only referred provider says D5 that no approved place exists before D45. A D9 case entry says “failed to engage” and omits the contact and provider response. The next review is D21. The exercise procedure directs requests to clarify, modify or enforce the order into the existing case, with the supplied filing and service requirements. An agency email alone does not amend the order.

**Evidence packet:** B1 order; B2 contact and receipt; B3 provider availability; B4 case entry; B5 fictional procedure card.

**Expected work:** Create separate Duty Cards for Jordan and the agency. Identify timely contact, apparent failure to provide the course, and incomplete characterization. Check for an additional referral or amended order. Request an actual approved option and record correction. Seek clarification, compliance and necessary deadline modification through the existing case without declaring the order void or assuming sanctions.

**Change card at minute 50:** The agency offers an approved course on D14 starting D18. The D9 “failed to engage” entry remains unchanged. The original D30 requirement and the D21 review remain in the order. What changes in your request?

**Facilitator key:** Update the trace: a service is now available, so a request for current provision may be partly satisfied. Verify the actual place, cost and any material access issue. Preserve accurate recording of the earlier barrier and determine whether timing clarification remains necessary. Do not continue claiming no service exists after receiving contrary evidence. Later compliance does not make the earlier entry accurate.

**Partial-remedy card at minute 79:** “Referral resent.” A resent referral is not proof of an available approved place or revised court instructions.

### Case C — A discrete agency duty has no recorded response

**Current day D24.** An invented federal office acknowledges a complete application D0. Its fictional rule requires a written determination within twenty days; a favorable determination depends separately on eligibility. On D24 the portal says pending and the supplied correspondence contains no determination. An online template claims silence creates consent, automatic damages and a guaranteed favorable court ruling. The packet supplies no special review statute, adequate alternative remedy analysis, exhaustion requirement, jurisdictional facts, service rules or exception analysis.

**Evidence packet:** C1 fictional statute card; C2 complete-application receipt; C3 current status; C4 unreliable template; C5 missing-information card.

**Expected work:** Name the precise possibly overdue act and its trigger; verify issuance, delivery and exceptions; distinguish a required determination from a required grant; reject invented consent or liability; research the actual review scheme before proposing a court filing. The real legal research anchor, *Norton v. Southern Utah Wilderness Alliance*, concerns discrete agency action legally required. It does not fill every missing judicial prerequisite.

**Change card at minute 50:** The Office produces a written denial transmitted on D19 to an incorrect address entered by the Office. It provides Taylor a copy on D25. The packet supplies no governing rule about notice or delivery, and no merits-review deadline. What must change in the trace?

**Facilitator key:** Do not persist in the original claim that no determination exists. Distinguish issuance, defective delivery and the merits of the denial. The requested process remedy may be partly satisfied, while questions about valid notice, review timing and substantive review remain open. Identify the actual source needed to resolve those questions. Do not invent tolling or assume an expired deadline.

**Partial-remedy card at minute 79:** “Written determination received; application denied.” The narrow objective of obtaining a determination may be satisfied. Whether the decision is correct and reviewable is a different trace.

## Facilitator answer method

Score what the learner can demonstrate, not whether the learner sounds legally sophisticated. Plain language is sufficient. A correctly identified unknown is better than a confident invented rule.

When a group makes an unsupported accusation, ask: “What observation supports that exact classification?” When it names a court, ask: “Which source gives that court authority over this requested relief?” When it proposes a notice, ask: “What provision makes this document a valid trigger?” When it claims completion, ask: “What changed in the active system and in the person's access?”

Read back ambiguous phrases. “The agency must do something” is too broad. “The specified officer must issue the written determination within the supplied period after a complete request” is a testable duty. “Enforce the Constitution” is not a sufficiently specific procedural request. “Issue the determination required by the identified source, if the forum has authority and prerequisites are met” defines a narrower question.

### Misroute test

Give each group one incorrect route and require a source-based correction.

| Incorrect proposition | Required response |
|---|---|
| “A state family court ignored me, so the federal APA supplies the appeal.” | The federal APA is not the universal review scheme for state courts; identify the existing state case and applicable review route. |
| “A public employee acted unfairly, so section 1983 automatically supplies damages.” | Identify a right, required legal elements, defendants, defenses and available relief; unfairness alone is not the whole analysis. |
| “My sworn statement was unanswered, so its allegations became binding.” | Identify an applicable procedural rule if one has that effect; private silence does not itself create consent or a binding judgment. |
| “The agency missed a deadline, so the applicant must be approved.” | Identify the consequence of missing that deadline; the duty to decide and entitlement to a favorable outcome are separate. |
| “I requested correction, so my appeal time stopped.” | Find the governing tolling rule. In Case A, the packet expressly says it does not stop. |
| “A corrected original automatically fixes every copy.” | Seek evidence of notification, receipt and operational change as appropriate; distinguish legal duties from additional checks. |

### Assessment rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 4: **0 absent or materially wrong; 1 mostly unsupported; 2 partly correct with significant gaps; 3 accurate and usable with a stated gap; 4 accurate, sourced, complete for the supplied packet and revised when evidence changes.**

| Dimension | Evidence for a score of 4 |
|---|---|
| Fact and evidence discipline | Specific act; accurate chronology; source IDs; independent versus copied evidence; inference separated from observation |
| Authority and predicates | Relevant source identified; scope and conditions correctly mapped; required act separated from discretion |
| Procedure and clocks | Correct trigger, recipient and document; filing/service distinctions; all supplied parallel deadlines preserved |
| Route and requested relief | Relief matches actor's powers; unknown prerequisites flagged; no invented judicial path or automatic remedy |
| Remedy verification | Narrow process remedy distinguished from broad objective; source, recipient, dependency and practical changes checked appropriately |
| Participation and revision | Privacy preserved; affected-person consequence considered; reasonable challenge accepted; new evidence changes the trace |

**Foundation completion target:** 18 of 24 points, including at least 3 on both Authority and Predicates and Route and Requested Relief. If either source-based dimension is below 3, provide a short revision exercise rather than excluding the learner from future learning. Do not award completion while the work still invents legal authority, automatic liability, or a deadline unsupported by the packet. This course completion is evidence of a practiced educational skill, not a professional license or certification of legal competence.

### Individual exit skill proof: five minutes

**New microcase:** A fictional Records Review Officer must acknowledge a correctly routed request within five training days. A request was correctly routed and received on D0; today is D4. A separate adverse-notice review deadline is D6. The packet says a records request does not pause that review deadline. It provides no court route.

Ask each learner to write:

1. The narrow classification supported today.
2. One Duty Card sentence with the actor, act, trigger and deadline.
3. One timely next step and one unknown route question.
4. Evidence that would prove completion of the narrow duty.

**Answer:** A response is pending but not overdue; acknowledgement is due D5. Preserve the separate review route by D6 and verify its requirements. The court route is unknown. An actual acknowledgement tied to the request proves the narrow acknowledgement duty, not a correction, reversal or favorable outcome.

**Transfer question:** “What changes on D7?” The acknowledgement may then be overdue on the supplied rule, but the separate review deadline may already have passed. The learner should investigate available options and governing exceptions rather than invent tolling or assume irreversible loss without checking the law.

## Instructor demonstration with a real source

After legal-source review, demonstrate how the source changes the analysis using Florida Statutes section 39.701(2)(d)3. Read the complete relevant current provision and its context. The core teaching distinction is a court finding that the agency failed to comply with case-plan obligations, followed by separate consequences expressed using “may” and “shall.” A participant's allegation does not itself satisfy the court-finding predicate. An order to show why a child cannot safely be returned is not itself a direction to return the child immediately.

Keep this worked statutory reading distinct from the fictional exercise rules. It is an illustration of extracting actors, predicates and consequences; it is not a conclusion about any real person's case. The source-review agent provides the verified text and version for the final publication.

### Continuing module sequence

1. **Module 01 — One decision, one trace:** the current foundation module.
2. **Module 02 — Read a source into predicates:** hierarchy, definitions, cross-references, exceptions and version dates.
3. **Module 03 — Route through an actual jurisdiction:** one carefully bounded administrative or court procedure with verified official forms and time computation.
4. **Module 04 — Build a reliable evidence packet:** originals, provenance, chronology, lawful access, redaction and factual declarations.
5. **Module 05 — Correction that reaches the consequence:** dependent decisions, implementation and closure evidence.
6. **Module 06 — Independent review and quality:** case calibration, error measurement, conflicts and the limits of a reviewer's role.

Keep the atlas modular: add a verified route only when its authority, actor, predicates, forms, service, deadlines, review and remedy fields are complete enough for the stated educational purpose. An empty or uncertain route should remain visibly incomplete.
