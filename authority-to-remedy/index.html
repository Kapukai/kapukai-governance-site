A CONCRETE DUTY TRACE

Separate “may” from “shall.”

Florida Statutes §39.701(2)(d)3 illustrates why the trigger and the exact legal verb belong in the same record.

Read the statute
IF / PREDICATE

The court finds the agency is not complying with the written case plan.

MAY / DISCRETIONARY

The court may hold the agency in contempt.

SHALL / REQUIRED RESPONSE

The court shall order the agency to submit compliance plans and require it to show why the child could not safely be returned home.

VERIFY / OUTPUT

Check the finding, written order, required submissions, and subsequent review. The provision does not itself order automatic return.
