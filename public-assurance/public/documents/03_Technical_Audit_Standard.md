# Accountable authority technical audit standard

PSA AA01 • Draft 0.1 • Voluntary unless lawfully adopted

## Scope and vocabulary

This standard tests a defined process from receipt of a material assertion through decision, execution, review and correction. It is not an ISO or NIST publication. Conformity is not proof that every decision is lawful or correct. Applicable law controls when an adopted requirement conflicts with law; record the conflict and its effect on the assessment.

SHALL identifies a requirement of this proposed standard. SHOULD identifies a recommended practice. MAY permits an option. Evidence means material relevant to an assessment; it is not synonymous with a proven fact. Independence means protection from material conflicts in the reviewed function, not absence of all institutional relationships.

Coupling means a documented dependency or exchange between functions. Decoupling means separating control where conflicts or fault propagation require it. Coherence means consistency among operative evidence, authority, decisions and execution, with genuine disagreements retained. Decoherence means a material inconsistency among those elements; no quantum claim is intended.

## Control family one Evidence and authority

AA-01 Record. Preserve the material assertion, original source, time, provenance and amendment history. Test by tracing sampled decisions back to source records. Evidence: original or authorized reliable copy, identifiers and change log.

AA-02 Classify. Distinguish reports, observations, inferences, disputed facts and formal findings. Preserve status on transfer. Test a copied allegation and a corrected finding. Evidence: labels, definitions and transfer records.

AA-03 Establish authority. Identify the decision-maker, applicable authority, required factual conditions and evidence standard. Test a decision against each condition. Evidence: authority references, delegation and recorded criteria.

AA-04 Verify. Record how evidence supports required conditions and how material contrary evidence was addressed. Do not substitute checklist completion for substantive examination. Evidence: findings and source references.

AA-05 Bound action. State scope, duration, responsible executor and review trigger. Test expiration and execution beyond an order's limits. Evidence: decision, execution log and review record.

## Assessment rule

For every control use: demonstrated, partially demonstrated, not demonstrated, not applicable, or withheld. Record evidence, scope, method, uncertainty and contrary evidence. A policy proves policy existence; it does not establish operational compliance. A sample supports only the conclusions justified by its design. Do not infer nationwide prevalence from selected complaints.

# Participation independence and correction

## Control family two Human access and incentives

AA-06 Protect participation. Offer a route to raise safety, disability, language, transport and cost barriers before avoidable disclosure or adverse classification. Evidence: intake procedure, accommodation records and disclosure assessments. Test a safety objection that is not a refusal of the underlying process.

AA-07 Examine incentives. Identify material payment conditions, beneficiaries and decision relationships. Evidence: contracts, rate schedules, compensation and conflict records. Payment establishes a relationship; a causal claim requires additional evidence.

AA-08 Enable independent review. Document reviewer competence, appointment, conflicts, access and corrective or referral powers. Evidence: appointment terms and a completed review. Test whether a reviewer can disagree without the audited body suppressing the finding.

## Control family three Correction and controlled exceptions

AA-09 Propagate correction. Identify dependent records and decisions after a material change. Each responsible recipient shall reassess its own action and record the outcome. Evidence: dependency register, notices and reassessment decisions.

AA-10 Verify closure. Close only when defined tasks are evidenced or explicitly marked unresolved. Evidence: acceptance criteria, completed tasks and independent verification. A forwarded email or acknowledgment alone is insufficient.

AA-11 Control exceptions. Record the authority, basis, limits, duration and review of each exception. Evidence: exception register and review outcome. Emergencies require their applicable lawful pathway; this standard imposes no single universal proof threshold.

AA-12 Preserve accountability. Record material access, disclosure and changes using proportionate tamper-evident controls, retention rules and protected access. Evidence: access logs, integrity checks and retention schedule. A hash detects a byte change; it does not establish truth or a trustworthy original.

## Required finding structure

Control ID; covered process; applicable version; finding; evidence references; test performed; affected population; uncertainty; institutional response; risk; responsible function; requested correction; acceptance criteria; verification owner. Preserve the distinction between technical nonconformity and legal violation.

## Review safeguards

At least one reviewer independent of the delivery team shall examine material adverse findings. Where technical findings implicate legal, clinical or child-safety questions, obtain relevant domain review. A technical panel shall not certify a legal outcome outside its competence.

# Coupling coherence and fault containment

## Control family four Architecture

AA-13 Dependency coupling. Every sampled consequential decision shall link to material evidence, authority and prior decisions. Changes to those dependencies shall trigger reassessment. Evidence: relationship map and an exercised change event.

AA-14 Functional decoupling. Separate review from material interests in the challenged outcome. Document appointment, removal, payment, evidence access and mitigations. A different title or department is not sufficient evidence of independence.

AA-15 Controlled exchange. Record purpose, authority, recipient, permitted use and minimum necessary disclosure. Identity verification alone shall not authorize location disclosure. Evidence: exchange agreement, permissions and transaction logs.

AA-16 Coherence checks. Test whether execution matches the operative decision and current supporting conditions. Record and resolve contradictions without erasing legitimate disagreement. Evidence: reconciliation results and exception register.

AA-17 Decoherence response. Assign each material inconsistency an owner, risk assessment, interim-action determination and deadline. Determine whether action continues, changes or pauses under applicable authority. Evidence: assigned incident and reasoned response.

AA-18 Acknowledge correction. Authorized recipients shall record receipt, affected dependencies and resulting action or reasoned no-change determination. Evidence: acknowledgments plus implementation records, not delivery receipts alone.

AA-19 Contain faults. A claim shall not gain evidentiary certainty through repetition alone. Identify independent corroboration separately from copies sharing a source. Evidence: provenance graph and sampled corroboration assessment.

## Required scenario testing

Test supported and unsupported allegations; copied sources; credible urgent danger; missing authority; expired action; conflicting records; inaccessible participation; threatened disclosure; payment conflicts; false-positive defect findings; disputed corrections; nonresponsive recipients; and necessary protection that must continue despite a separate record error. Start with synthetic data.

For every scenario record the expected action, limits, disclosure permissions, support, review and correction state. Preserve an unresolved state with an owner and deadline. Never require a binary label where evidence does not support one.

## Output states

Possible outputs include bounded authorization, voluntary support, further verification, lawful emergency response, referral, protected participation, independent review, amendment, reaffirmation, termination through the proper process and unresolved escalation. The correct output is conditional on the domain, evidence and authority; no algorithm in this standard decides individual custody or guilt.

# Domain profiles and release governance

## Required profile fields

Each adopter shall complete: process boundary; population and exclusions; responsible legal entities; applicable laws and orders; decision thresholds; emergency pathway; access permissions; retention schedule; response deadlines; review route; correction powers; reporting limits; and escalation recipient. Obtain approval from a person authorized to adopt the profile.

## Domains requiring separate profiles

Emergency response — dispatch classification, routing, recording, transfer and safe continuity. Do not delay emergency response to complete an audit.

Police and criminal justice — source attribution, restrictive decisions, investigative records and correction, with applicable criminal procedure and disclosure limits.

Family and paternity proceedings — findings, testing and participation, custody and sensitive disclosures under the governing jurisdiction. Technical review does not amend an order.

Child protection — removal, placement continuation, review, payment conditions and reunification processes. Protect both the child and family participation.

Psychiatric and hospital care — detention, treatment authority, reassessment, discharge and consent. Include clinical expertise and emergency safeguards.

Adult guardianship — incapacity basis, delegated powers, financial interests and restoration procedures.

Education — disciplinary classifications, referrals, records sharing and accommodations.

Military and veterans systems — separate service and benefit processes, lawful access and review routes. Include the Coast Guard where relevant; do not assume identical branch rules.

Immigration and border systems — identity matching, restrictions, detention, disclosure and correction.

Benefits and housing — disputed eligibility records, adverse decisions, review and restoration.

## Measurement and change control

Predefine denominators, sampling, inclusion criteria and limitations. Track both missed protection and unnecessary restriction; privacy exposure; burden; time to review; rate of independent agreement; and verified correction completion. Never label a sample-based estimate an audit of every case.

Release records shall contain version, date, authorship, reviewer roles, adoption status, change log and unresolved objections. Preserve prior versions. Publish a vulnerability and error-reporting route. Changes require documented rationale and review; a safety-critical change may use an expedited recorded process followed by retrospective review.

Self-assessment, independent assessment and accreditation are different claims. This draft establishes no accreditation body. Adopters shall not use NIST, ISO or government marks or imply affiliation without authorization.
