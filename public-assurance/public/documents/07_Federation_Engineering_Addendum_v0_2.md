# Public Systems Assurance
## Draft 0.2 addendum — 27 September 2026

This proposed addendum supplements, but does not silently replace, the six-document Draft 0.1 founding package. No office, tribunal, accreditation or compulsory authority is created by publication.

### AA-20 — Federated correction
Participating institutions SHALL support authenticated correction notices, preserve provenance and version history, assign responsibility, and record reasoned responses. Authorized recipients SHALL reassess material dependencies. An independent reviewer SHALL verify completion. Unresolved disputes SHALL remain visible to authorized participants and follow a defined escalation process. Acknowledgment, corrective action and independently verified completion SHALL be separate states. Public reporting SHALL protect private information.

### AA-21 — Federated review and adjudication
Each participating body SHALL disclose its jurisdiction, appointment process, funding, conflicts, evidentiary rules, available remedies, and appeal route. Authentication SHALL NOT substitute for evidence. Advisory findings SHALL be distinguished from binding decisions. No body SHALL acquire coercive authority solely through federation membership. Correction completion SHALL require verification independent of the institution responsible for the error. Reviewer selection, removal, evidence access and publication restrictions SHALL be reviewable. Technical reviewers SHALL NOT silently modify source records or override judicial orders.

### Technical leadership
The proposed Chief Engineer for Public Systems Assurance SHALL demonstrate the ability to integrate accessible human communication, source-code inspection, data science, systems architecture, and electrical or control engineering. Competence may be supplied by a multidisciplinary team under qualified leadership. Qualifications SHALL be demonstrated through relevant work and practical assessment, not title alone.

Reviews SHALL trace reported consequences through source records, implemented rules, software, decisions and institutional handoffs. Reports SHALL identify evidence, uncertainty, methods, limitations and correction criteria. Independent review SHALL evaluate the Office's own work. Legal consequences remain with duly authorized decision-makers and review bodies.

### Adoption and pilot
An authorized pilot agreement should specify scope, participants, access limits, review independence, response deadlines, correction responsibilities, costs and escalation. Start with synthetic cases; use real records only under documented authorization and privacy controls. Measure acknowledgment time, decision time, downstream completion and practical resolution. A permanent office with compulsory powers requires the appropriate legal authority and funding.

### Website participation boundary
The optional website form records general inquiries and expressly selected update preferences. It is not the protected case-review form, a legal filing, a research consent form, paid enrollment or an appointment to a review role. The Draft 0.1 forms remain downloadable documents and are not transmitted by downloading them.
